# Demo guide

A 10-minute tour of Catchami at **[catchami.fun](https://catchami.fun/)**. It works on a phone (best for catching) or a computer (arrow keys / WASD to move, and the game shows a QR code to catch with your phone). Everything runs on NEAR testnet with test coins.

## 1. Open the game and sign in
Open **catchami.fun** → **Start game** → skip or watch the short story.
- **Play as guest** or **Continue with Google**: one tap, nothing to install.
- **Connect a NEAR wallet** (Meteor, Intear, HERE or Sender): if you want pets minted straight into your own wallet.

## 2. Get started with coins
Pick a **starter pouch** (Sprout 10M, Bloom 25M or Grove 60M). On the test network no payment is taken. The coins are real testnet $CATCHAMI delivered on-chain from the market allocation.

## 3. Catch an animal
Point your camera at a **real cat or dog** until the yellow box turns green and locks on, then flick or tap to throw the **CamiCube**. Your first catch always succeeds.
- Nothing is charged if there's no animal, the picture is a still photo, or it's a duplicate.
- On a computer, scan the QR code to open the camera on your phone.

## 4. Meet your pet NFT and its wallet
The **Caught!** card shows your pet's rarity, traits and **piggy bank**: 40% of the cube price is now your pet's own money. Name your pet. Within seconds the game mints it as an NFT on `game.catchami-dev.testnet`.

## 5. Learn the ropes
A short tutorial in the Keeper's Glade walks you through moving, gathering, selling, buying meals and medicine, crafting, a practice battle and the marketplace. When it ends, step into the portal to Paw Village.

## 6. Gather resources
Head into **Whispering Forest** (wood, herbs, fish) or **Mining Valley** (stone). Walk up to a spot and tap **Gather!**, then play the minigame: perfect timing doubles the yield. Watch the bag, energy and hunger bars: work costs energy and makes your pet hungry.

## 7. Sell to a travelling merchant
Find **Finn**, **Sage Marigold**, **Timber Tom** or **Rocky Ruby** on the roads (tap them to walk over) → **Open shop** → **Sell everything**. The shop shows each merchant's real purse, cart space and today's prices. The coins go straight into your pet's piggy bank. The **Demand** bar at the top (▲ / ▼) shows which resources are in demand.

## 8. Buy food and medicine
In the same shop, buy a **Meal** (7K) when hungry and **Medicine** (35K) when hurt. Meals are cooked from fish merchants actually delivered to HQ. If the kitchen runs out, an imported meal costs double.

## 9. Bubble Roulette
In the Forest or Mining Valley, rival pets may challenge yours to **Bubble Roulette**, or you can walk up to one and tap **⚔️ Battle**.
- The launcher holds 6 bubbles, 3 of them loaded.
- On your turn, splash yourself or your rival. An empty self-splash gives you 2 extra shots, and two soaks end the match.
- Against demo rivals, only bag contents are at stake.
- A soaked pet drops to 0 HP and needs medicine before it can gather again.

## 10. Craft gear
At any merchant, tap **Open shop → Gear**. Craft items like **Trail Boots** (+6% speed) from wood and stone; any missing materials can be bought from HQ stock. Then equip them in **Profile → Gear**.

## 11. Set up Autopilot
Open **Profile → 🤖 Autopilot** and pick the effort level, which resource to gather (or "highest demand"), and how to sell. Then stop steering: your pet gathers, sells, eats, buys medicine and naps on its own, even when you're offline.

## 12. Check your money and the chain
- **Profile → History:** every coin your pet earned and spent.
- **Profile → Piggy bank:** the pet's NFT ID and its on-chain balance.
- **Coin Vault** (tap your coin balance → Open wallet):
  - **$CATCHAMI on-chain:** the latest settlement batch, and a **⚡ Sync now** button.
  - **Buy · Withdraw · Trade:** buying coins (payment simulated, delivery real), withdrawing a pet's coins to your NEAR wallet, and swapping $CATCHAMI for test USDC. Withdraw and Trade need a connected NEAR wallet.
  - **Where do coins come from?:** HQ's treasury and today's merchant budget.
