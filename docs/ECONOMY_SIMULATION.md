# Catchami economy simulation

Generated by `pnpm sim` on 2026-11-13T11:02:28.283Z with economy config `2026-11-13.1`.

Model: 30-minute steps over 30 days. Same catch odds, splits, prices, demand index, treasury taper, battle engine as the live server. Resource output is capped by the real map (9 fish nodes, 10 herbs nodes, 12 wood nodes, 16 minerals nodes) and merchant cart capacity. Money uses an exact double-entry ledger; invariants are checked every simulated day.

Population mix: 50% casual, 22% grinders, 5% whales (bigger deposits, aggressive battles, weekly withdrawals), 8% bot-like farm accounts (minimum deposit, withdraw anything above 800K), 15% dormant owners (never return; autopilot keeps running).
